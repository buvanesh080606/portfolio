# V S Buvanesh Raj — Dynamic Portfolio Website

Interactive GSAP-powered personal portfolio for **V S Buvanesh Raj** — B.E. CSE (AI & ML) undergraduate at Saranathan College of Engineering & Full-Stack Developer.

**Live Website:** https://buvanesh080606.github.io/portfolio/

## Highlights
- **Kinetic Hero & Tech Stack Toggle:** Interactive GSAP SplitText hero and animated technology marquee backdrop.
- **Scroll-Driven About Me & Flower Showcase:** Smooth Lenis + ScrollTrigger animation with quick links to Resume and Contact.
- **Selected Projects:** Overseas Recruitment Management System, FreshVerse AI, BloodChain AI, VigilOps AI, and Ocean3D.
- **Interactive My Journey Roadmap:** Pinned winding SVG road on desktop/tablet and vertical timeline on mobile.
- **Services & Direct Contact:** Interactive cloud mascot contact form wired to deliver messages directly to `vsbuvaneshraj06@gmail.com`.
- **Interactive & Downloadable Resume:** Dedicated [resume.html](./resume.html) page plus downloadable ATS PDF ([V_S_Buvanesh_Raj_Resume.pdf](./assets/V_S_Buvanesh_Raj_Resume.pdf)).
